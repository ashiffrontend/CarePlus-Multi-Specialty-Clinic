Emergency First Aid Protocol Cards

Chest Pain Protocol

Keep the patient calm in a seated position. Loosen tight garments around the neck. Do not exert effort. Immediately call 1800-CARE-911.

Stroke Signs (F.A.S.T.)

Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call. Note the exact time symptoms began for clot-busting therapies.

Severe Bleeding

Apply firm, direct pressure using a clean cloth. Elevate the bleeding limb above heart level if no fracture is suspected.

Burns & Scalds

Cool the burn with running tap water for 15-20 minutes. Do not apply ice, butter, or paste. Cover gently with sterile gauze.
